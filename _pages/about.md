 Hello! I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Psychology at the [University of Notre Dame](https://psychology.nd.edu/people/faculty/jingnan-du/), where I direct the Precision Brain Mapping Lab. My research focuses on the organization of large-scale human brain networks and their roles in higher-order cognitive functions, such as language processing, social inference, working memory, and episodic memory. By combining within-individual neuroimaging with computational methods, I characterize parallel networks and their functional specialization within association zones across the cerebral cortex, cerebellum, striatum, and thalamus. I developed the [DU15NET atlases](https://freesurfer.net/fswiki/CorticalParcellation_DU15NET), a 15‑network parcellation distributed with FreeSurfer. I also collaborate with clinicians to map individualized brain networks, and use these personalized maps to refine neurosurgical planning and neuromodulation targets. Before joining Notre Dame, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the [Buckner Lab](https://bucknerlab.fas.harvard.edu/) at Harvard University. I completed my PhD at Fudan University and spent the final two years of my doctoral training as a joint PhD student in the Department of Psychology at Harvard University. Outside the lab, I play basketball and spend a lot of time looking for [birds to photograph](/birds/).
